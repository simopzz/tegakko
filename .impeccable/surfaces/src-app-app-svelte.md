# Practice app redesign

Scope: full web PWA redesign, Operate mode. Audience: solo Japanese learners in a quiet morning session on a portrait stylus tablet. The job is to choose what to practise, write from memory, self-grade, and reflect on the session. Success is a low-friction, focused practice ritual with honest feedback and clear local ownership. Preserve current content, session behavior, blank recall canvas, weakness-biased sampling, pressure-sensitive ink, Undo, backup semantics, and accessibility status announcements. No gamification, remote account, cloud sync, tracking, or new learning claims. Six screens are in scope: First Run, Scope Picker, Drill, Session Summary, History, and Settings.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Tegakko is a well-kept morning writing desk: clear sheets for decisions, a blank cell for recall, and a bookmark that keeps the learner oriented. Refuse generic card dashboards and decorative notebook cosplay.

**OWN-WORLD:** Use the selected sketch’s smooth paper, ink, deep green, and terracotta; apply them across surfaces and status roles. Familiar app navigation and controls stay recognizable. Grouped sheets have quiet edges and a cloth bookmark tab identifies the active scope. Type is legible first; authentic Japanese glyphs may carry the expressive voice. No texture or ornament enters the writing cell.

**STORY:** First Run explains the optional stylus check; Scope Picker names scripts, relevant groups or JLPT level, and session length in plain language; Drill brings prompt and blank canvas forward; Summary reflects on the work before accuracy; History makes change interpretable; Settings separates writing feel, idle close, and backup.
