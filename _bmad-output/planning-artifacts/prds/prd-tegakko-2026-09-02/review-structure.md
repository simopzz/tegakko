**Structure model:** Strategic/Context (Pyramid) — per `structure-models.md`, PRDs map to this model (top-down conclusion, logically grouped support, most-critical-first ordering, MECE groupings, evidence-not-leading). Evaluated against: does the headline (what v1 is/isn't) surface early and unambiguously, is supporting detail grouped without overlap, and does nothing repeat without reinforcement value.
